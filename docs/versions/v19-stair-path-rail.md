# v19: the stair path and blue rail lashings

**Goal.** Refine the walking route seen from the beginning of the descent through the lower
stairs. The paved start should show chipped, weathered, uneven steps rather than a repeated
perfect flight. The ridge and lower path should change width and tread condition naturally.
Blue cord should bind the rough bamboo rail to its posts with snug crossed wraps, compact
knots, and short ends instead of loose circular loops.

Judge at walking distance from several positions on the stairs and ridge, including the
first paved flight and the low dirt steps. Compare Sam's 2026-09-29 render and supplied
photos with `references/03-trail/`, especially the start of the descent and mid descent,
and the close railing in the [Kelingking hike photo](https://www.letsventureout.com/kelingking-beach-hike-nusa-penida/).

## Budget and limits

- Keep the walking line continuous and the scroll camera clear of the rail.
- Keep the frame time within the project's 10% budget at all hero shots.
- Preserve the island silhouette, sea, and clouds from v18.
