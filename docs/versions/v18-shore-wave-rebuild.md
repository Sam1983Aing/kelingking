# v18: natural shore break

**Goal.** Rebuild the breaking wave at Kelingking Beach so its crest steepens, spills,
collapses and reaches the sand in one convincing motion. Preserve the water's current
colour and fine surface texture; the geometric tube and its handoff to whitewater are
the focus of this version.

## Judge it in motion

- Compare fixed-camera clips at `shoreBreak`, `swash`, `beach` and `surfTop` with v17.
  Follow one crest from its first steepening to its foam spreading and draining.
- From the overview, the breaker should trace the beach without a squared-off corner
  or a single uniform white bar.
- Keep the v17 viewpoint and headland outline. No hero frame over 10% slower than v17
  in a paired run.

## Current weakness

The curled ribbon reads as a smooth blue tube before it breaks, then the whitewater
appears as a separate band. At the waterline the crest and foam do not seem to share
one continuous loss of energy. Sam's 2026-09-28 screenshots show the hard-edged surf
line from the air. The motion notes in the referenced Hajime Tsui post call out jitter
and whitewater that keeps its shape as it slides onto sand.

## Scope

The near-shore wave geometry and the transition into existing surf foam and swash.
Do not change the open-ocean spectrum, terrain, clouds, plants or cliff silhouette.
