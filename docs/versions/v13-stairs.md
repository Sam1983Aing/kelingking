# v13: the dirt steps and the bamboo handrail

**Goal.** From Sam, on a screenshot from the descent, after v12: the blue cord that joins the
bamboo looks "very below average", and the stairs look too polygon. Make both more realistic.

Judge them close up, from where someone walking would look: `capture.mjs trailLow --eval=` with
`__app.contactSheet` at cameras on the route (`s` metres along it, 178 to 304 is the descent,
dirt steps and bamboo rails), and cameras aimed at single lashings. Against
`references/03-trail/trail-mid-descent-b.jpg`, `trail-top-railing.jpg` and
`trail-low-beach-close.jpg`.

## As it was at v12

1. **The cord** was one smooth blue cylinder 7 cm tall round each end of each rail (a collar).
2. **The steps** (the dirt treads of the descent): five or six large flat quads per tread,
   a razor front edge, a flat riser, a straight ramp down each side, flat shading. Each read
   as a slab.
3. **The bamboo** poles were straight, even tubes with a faint ring.
4. Two rails meeting at a post were on the same line, one running through the other.
