# Kelingking references

- Cliff about 180 m high
- Top of trail: 156 concrete steps, then a steep dirt and rock path with a low bamboo handrail for roughly two thirds of the way
- Beach: white sand, strong shore break, swimming banned since 2023
- Layout from satellite: the ridge runs from the viewpoint (NE) south-west and hooks west into the T-Rex head. The beach sits inside the hook, facing NW. Small islet (Batu Rananja) to the south.
- Unfinished glass lift shaft and crane on the cliff since 2025. Leave it out.

Satellite: https://www.google.com/maps/@-8.7512,115.4718,350m/data=!3m1!1e3
